How This Works

Each scene needs you to say something real — comfort a friend, give a stranger directions, describe a sunset to someone who can't see it.

Type freely. Small joining words (a, the, and, to…) are always free. Everything else you type — every distinct word — is spent the moment you send it, and it never comes back. Typos are forgiven; the meaning is what matters.

Your keyboard remembers what you've already spent. It will not offer those words to you again — not tomorrow, not ever.

When the thousand run out, there is one last scene. It asks for nothing.
